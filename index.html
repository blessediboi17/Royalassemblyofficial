IMAGE OF FOUNDER

General Overseer & Founder

With a divine mandate to raise a generation of excellence, our General Overseer has dedicated over two decades to the ministry of the Gospel. Known for a profound teaching grace and a heart for humanity, the Founder established Royal Assembly with the vision of spiritual and academic liberation.

Under their leadership, the ministry has expanded globally, birthing the Royal Academy School System (RASS) to ensure that the leaders of tomorrow are anchored in both faith and knowledge.
